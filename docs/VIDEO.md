# AeroLab demo video: script and submission notes

Target: under 2 minutes. Show the A-vs-B result clearly. Say the
"before spending money on a prototype" line.

## What to say

### Why I built it

> I didn't want to build something that's only cool. I wanted something
> productive, something an industry can actually use. Car and aircraft
> companies pay a lot for wind tunnels and prototypes. I built one you put
> on your desk.

### What it is

> VIRTUFORGE AeroLab is a wind tunnel in VR. It runs in the browser on
> Meta Quest, with your hands only, no controllers.

### Show it (one flow, nothing extra)

1. Pick the car. Slide the lever.
   > Real airflow physics, live.
2. Pinch a handle, drag the roof down.
   > The shape changes, the air changes with it.
3. Press **Save A**, lower the tail, show the results panel
   ("B has X% less drag than A").
   > Design, change, compare. In seconds, before you build anything.
4. Drop in your own model, drag a part.
   > Any model. Your model.

### The advantage, in one line

> A designer can test an idea in ten minutes, for free, before spending
> money on a physical prototype.

### Close

> Open it today in the Quest Browser. Link in the description.

## How to record

1. Record in the headset: Meta button, Camera, Record.
   For better quality, use Meta Quest Developer Hub on the PC
   (Device, Cast / Record) with the headset on USB.
2. Record your voice on a phone or PC in a quiet room. Read the lines above.
3. Put them together in CapCut or Clipchamp. Export 1080p MP4.

## How to submit

1. YouTube, Upload video.
2. Visibility: **Unlisted** (not Private, or judges cannot open it).
3. Title: `VIRTUFORGE AeroLab - wind tunnel in VR`
4. Description:

   ```
   VIRTUFORGE AeroLab is a wind tunnel in VR for Meta Quest. Reshape cars,
   planes and wings with your hands, import your own model, watch live
   airflow and compare design A vs B in seconds. No controllers.

   App:  https://abc-001-f.github.io/virtuforge/
   Code: https://github.com/ABC-001-f/virtuforge
   By Achonu Bonaventure Chibuike
   ```

5. Copy the YouTube link into the competition form, together with the app
   link and the code link above.
